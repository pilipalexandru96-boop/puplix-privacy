Privacy Policy for Puplix

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Puplix handles information when you use the game.

Puplix is developed and published by the app developer.

1. Information collected

Puplix does not directly collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address.

However, third-party services integrated into the game may collect certain information automatically in order to provide their functionality.

These services may include:

  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

Depending on the service and your device settings, information collected by these third parties may include:

  • device and app information
  • advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • approximate location derived from IP address
  • diagnostic and performance information
  • interaction with advertisements
  • Google Play Games account information required for sign-in and leaderboard functionality

2. Google Play Games Services

Puplix uses Google Play Games Services for features such as:

  • Google account sign-in
  • leaderboards
  • score submission

Google may process information associated with your Google Play Games profile according to Google's own privacy policies.

Puplix does not receive your Google account password.

3. Advertising

Puplix uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect and process data in order to:

  • deliver advertisements
  • measure ad performance
  • prevent fraud and abuse
  • provide personalized or non-personalized ads where permitted

Where required by law, Puplix uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before certain advertising data is used.

You may be given options to consent to, reject, or manage certain advertising and privacy choices.

4. Consent and privacy choices

For users in regions where consent is required, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, a privacy message may be shown when the app starts.

Your consent choices are handled through Google's User Messaging Platform.

Where available, you may be able to review or change your privacy choices from within the app.

5. Children's privacy

Puplix is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If the game is distributed to audiences that include children, advertising and privacy settings will be configured in accordance with applicable platform and advertising requirements.

6. Data retention

Puplix does not maintain its own server database containing your personal information.

Data collected by third-party services is retained according to the policies and retention practices of those services.
